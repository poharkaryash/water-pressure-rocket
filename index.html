THE QUESTION WE ARE ANSWERING

Can a rocket made from reused soft drink bottles, with three extra bottles storing compressed air, launch reliably and fly steadily?

SEC03

How it works

  1. Fill

    Water goes into the main 2-litre bottle.

  2. Pressurise

    Air is pumped in and stored in the three 1-litre bottles.

  3. Release

    The nozzle on the launch pad is released and the pressure pushes the water out.

  4. Lift off

    Water goes down fast, and the rocket goes up. Every action has an equal and opposite reaction.

SAFETY

Wear eye protection, launch outdoors, keep everyone well back, and never pressurise the bottles beyond what they can safely hold.
